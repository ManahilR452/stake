HACKSUMMER’26 BRIEF 
Theme: Trust Me 
Objective 
Build technology that helps people decide what, who, or which information they can trust. Projects may 
address verification, transparency, fraud, identity, misinformation, explainable AI, cybersecurity, or digital 
trust. 
Tip: Keep asking yourself “Does this solution actually improve trust?”

Criteria Definitions 
● Theme Relevance & Impact  
Genuine differentiation 
Working, coherent demo 
Substantive engineering 
Real-world viability 
How directly and meaningfully does the solution address trust, verification, transparency, fraud, 
identity, misinformation, cybersecurity, etc.? 
● Innovation / Novelty  
Is the idea, approach, or implementation meaningfully different from obvious existing solutions? 
● Execution & Demo  
Does the core product actually work? Is the user flow coherent? Does the demo prove the claimed 
functionality? 
● Technical Depth  
Is there meaningful engineering behind the solution - APIs, models, algorithms, architecture, security 
mechanisms, data processing, etc.?  
Note: this is scored as Technical Depth, not Technical Complexity. Complexity for its own sake does 
not earn marks. 
● Feasibility 
Could this realistically work beyond the hackathon? Are the assumptions, data requirements, 
scalability, deployment, and user adoption reasonable?
